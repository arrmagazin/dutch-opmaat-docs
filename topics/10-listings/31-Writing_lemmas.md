# Content Lemmas

All non-auxiliary (content-word) lemmas from [30-Writing_sentences.md](/#/topics?doc=10-listings/30-Writing_sentences.md) and [32-Speaking_answers.md](/#/topics?doc=10-listings/32-Speaking_answers.md): nouns, main verbs, adjectives, lexical adverbs, months and weekdays. Function words (articles, pronouns, prepositions, conjunctions, particles), auxiliary/modal verbs, numerals, and proper nouns are excluded. 591 lemmas.

The topics of both source lists are converged into the single set below — the writing list's situations (appointments, repairs, complaints, emails) merged with the speaking list's themes, plus three cross-topic groups for words that belong to no single subject. Each group is sorted A→Z.
