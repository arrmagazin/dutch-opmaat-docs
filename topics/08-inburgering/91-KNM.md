## non-Gov Institutions

- `Juridisch Loket` : Legal questions
- `Huurcommissie`: Unfair rent — independent body for disputes between huurder and verhuurder (rent price, overdue maintenance) [Q94]
- `Makelaar`  Buy a house
- `Woningcorporatie`  Rent social housing [Q51]
- `Huisarts`  Health problem (non-emergency)
- `Apotheek`  Prescription medicine (with recept) [Q116]
- `Buurtvereniging` : residents who organize activities and keep the neighbourhood liveable [Q133]
- `Huismeester` : maintains a block of flats and is the contact point for residents [Q154]

- Een `uitzendbureau` bemiddelt tussen werkzoekenden en bedrijven. Je werkt dan tijdelijk bij een bedrijf via het uitzendbureau. [Q81]
- Een `vakbond` behartigt de belangen van werknemers, bijvoorbeeld bij onderhandelingen over loon en arbeidsvoorwaarden (CAO). [Q83]
- Een `ZZP`'er (Zelfstandige Zonder Personeel) is iemand die voor zichzelf werkt en geen werknemers in dienst heeft. Een ZZP'er is een ondernemer. [Q84]
- Het `MBO` (Middelbaar Beroepsonderwijs) is een praktijkgerichte opleiding na het VMBO. Je leert daar een vak, zoals verpleging, techniek of administratie. [Q97]
- Een `CAO` (Collectieve Arbeidsovereenkomst) is een overeenkomst tussen werkgevers en vakbonden over arbeidsvoorwaarden zoals loon, werktijden en vakantiedagen voor een hele sector. [Q121]
- Het `CBR` (Centraal Bureau Rijvaardigheidsbewijzen) is de organisatie die rijexamens afneemt en rijbewijzen verstrekt in Nederland. [Q127]

## Constitution & rights

- **Grondwet**: the most important law of the Netherlands; it contains the grondrechten (fundamental rights) of citizens [Q140]
- **Artikel 1 Grondwet**: everyone in the Netherlands is treated equally; discrimination on any ground (religion, race, gender, sexual orientation, etc.) is forbidden [Q11]
- **Vrijheid van meningsuiting** (free speech): you may state your opinion — including satire and criticism of politics and religion — but you may **not** incite hatred (haatzaaien), threaten violence, or discriminate [Q19, Q141]
- **Vrijheid van godsdienst**: everyone may choose their own religion, or no religion at all [Q24]
- **Scheiding van kerk en staat**: government and religion are separate; the state is neutral and not connected to any religion. You may not force your beliefs on others [Q17, Q142]
- **Gelijkheid van man en vrouw**: men and women are equal by law. A husband may **not** forbid his wife from working or going out — she decides for herself [Q15]
- **Discriminatie op de werkvloer** is forbidden — on grounds of origin, gender, religion, age or disability [Q124]
- **Vrijheid van onderwijs** is in the Grondwet; both openbare and bijzondere schools are largely paid for by the government [Q139]

- De `Rijksoverheid` is de landelijke overheid van Nederland, geleid door de minister-president en ministers. Zij maken landelijk beleid. [Q89]

Government & Parliament — counts

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|
| Tweede Kamer members | **150** (directly elected by the population) [Q61] |
| Eerste Kamer members | **75** (elected by Provinciale Staten) [Q62] |
| Provinces | **12** (e.g., Noord-Holland, Zuid-Holland, Utrecht, Gelderland, Limburg) [Q66] |
| Bestuurslagen (layers of government) | **3** — gemeente, provincie, Rijksoverheid [Q128] |
| Tweede Kamer election cycle | Every **4 years** (sooner if the kabinet falls) [Q143] |

| Role / body | Function |

| Minister-president | Chairs the ministerraad (kabinet) and leads the government [Q102] |

Civic termins

- **Voting age**: 18 — applies to all elections (Tweede Kamer, gemeenteraad, etc.) [Q63]
- **Gemeenteraadsverkiezingen** also open to:
- EU citizens aged 18+ [Q104]
- Non-EU citizens after **5 years** of uninterrupted, legal residence in NL [Q104]
- **Identificatieplicht** (ID duty): everyone aged **14+** must show valid ID on demand by police or authorized officials [Q130]
- **BRP registration** after moving to a new gemeente: within **5 days** [Q49]
- **BSN** (burgerservicenummer): unique personal number, issued when you register with a gemeente; needed for government, healthcare and work [Q46]
- **DigiD**: your digital identity for logging in online at government bodies (Belastingdienst, DUO, zorgverzekeraar) [Q47]
- **KNM-examen** (Kennis van de Nederlandse Maatschappij) is a compulsory part of the inburgeringsexamen [Q115]

## Insurance & health, Employment — leave, pay, deadlines

- **Zorgverzekering** (basic health insurance) is compulsory from age **18**;
- children under 18 are co-insured for free [Q38]
- No insurance → first a warning, then a **boete**, and finally the CAK insures you automatically [Q119]
- **Eigen risico**: the amount you pay yourself before the insurer pays — does **not** apply to the huisarts [Q39]
- **Tandartskosten** (dentist) covered by basic insurance **only for children under 18** [Q78]
- **Kraamzorg** (postnatal home care): first **8–10 days** after birth [Q118]

| Right / rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Minimum vacation days/year | **4 × workdays per week** (5-day week → 20 days) [Q82] |
| Loondoorbetaling bij ziekte (sick pay) | Employer pays salary up to **2 years** [Q85] |
| Zwangerschaps- en bevallingsverlof (maternity leave) | Minimum **16 weeks**; begins **4–6 weeks** before due date [Q120] |
| Kinderbijslag (child benefit) | Per child until age **18** (paid by SVB) [Q90] |

Care providers

- Health questions: First contact is always the huisarts (unless it's an emergency → 112) [Q37, Q158]
- Psychological complaints also go to the huisarts first, who can refer you to the **GGZ** [Q77]
- `Huisartsenpost`: the GP practice outside office hours — evening, night and weekend — for urgent complaints [Q76]
- `Tandarts`: checks and treats your gebit (teeth); a check-up twice a year is usual [Q80]
- `Verloskundige` (midwife): guides pregnant women before, during and after the birth; many Dutch women give birth at home [Q117]
- `Rijksvaccinatieprogramma`: children are vaccinated **free of charge** against serious diseases (mazelen, difterie) [Q79]
- You arrange it yourself: huisarts, tandarts

Work — terms

- **Arbeidsovereenkomst**: contract between employer and employee covering salary, working hours and tasks [Q42]
- **Proeftijd**: short trial period at the start of a contract in which either side may end it immediately [Q44]
- **Minimumloon**: the lowest wage an employer must legally pay, set by the government [Q41]
- **Brutoloon vs nettoloon**: bruto is before tax and premiums; netto is what actually reaches your bank account [Q125]
- **Ontslag**: register as a jobseeker with the UWV as soon as possible; UWV decides on your WW [Q123]
- **Vrijwilligerswerk**: unpaid work for the community (sports club, school, buurtvereniging) — highly valued in NL [Q122]

## Social norms & values

- **Afspraak maken**: you make an appointment before visiting someone. Turning up unannounced is generally impolite, even with close friends [Q12]
- **Stiptheid** (punctuality) matters, socially and professionally. Being late without calling is rude [Q13, Q75]
- **Directheid**: the Dutch say what they think and give an honest opinion. It is not meant rudely — at work you are expected to disagree openly, even with your boss [Q14]
- **Overleg / poldermodel**: decisions are made together through consultation and consensus — at work, in politics and in daily life [Q33, Q148]
- **Gezelligheid**: cosy, convivial togetherness — a café, dinner or gathering can be *gezellig*. A core Dutch value [Q34]
- **"Doe maar gewoon, dan doe je al gek genoeg"**: modesty and level-headedness; do not show off [Q32, Q111]
- **Open curtains** in the evening is a tradition of openness — but it does not mean you may stare inside [Q31]
- **U vs jij**: `u` is formal (older people, strangers, authority); `jij`/`je` is informal. Many Dutch workplaces use `jij` even with the boss [Q35]
- **Handdruk** (handshake) is the standard greeting in professional/formal settings; you say your name while shaking hands [Q18]
- Among friends and family, the Dutch greet with drie zoenen (three kisses on the cheeks: right-left-right). A handshake is used in formal/professional settings. [Q21]

Relationships & life events

- **Samenwonen** unmarried is allowed; no `samenlevingscontract` is required, but report it to the gemeente [Q6, Q9]
- **Geregistreerd partnerschap**: a registered partnership giving nearly the same legal rights as marriage [Q22]
- **Burgerlijk huwelijk** must legally take place at the **gemeente**; a religious ceremony is optional and has no legal status on its own [Q23]
- **Geboortekaartje**: birth announcement card sent to family and friends (name, date of birth, weight) [Q28]
- **Condoleancekaart**: condolence card sent to the family when someone has died [Q29]
- At age **50**, friends and family traditionally place a life-size figure of **Abraham** (men) or **Sarah** (women) at the front door — a biblical reference to old age [Q27]
