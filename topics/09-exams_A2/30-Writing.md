# Writing exam (Schrijven) A2 — samples & how to pass

## How the exam works

- **4 tasks** (opgaven), always the same three kinds: a **form**, two-to-three **e-mails / notes**, and one short **wijkkrant** text.
- **~35–40 minutes** for all four together — about **8–10 minutes per task**. Time is tight; do not overthink.
- The real DUO exam is on a **computer (you type)**. The downloadable practice booklets are the paper version — the tasks are identical, only the input differs.
- The exam is from the **Ministerie van SZW / DUO** and is secret. The practice booklets are the public equivalents; use them.
- **"Bedenk zelf"** = *make it up yourself*. Where the task says this, invent simple, plausible details (a name, a date, a reason). Nobody checks if it is true.

### How it is scored

Roughly **37 points** total: the **form ≈ 7 points**, each of the **other three ≈ 10 points**. As a study target, aim for **~25/37** to feel safe (the official pass mark is not published).

Each e-mail / text / note is judged on:

| Criterion | Points | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| **Adequaatheid / begrijpelijkheid** | 0–3 | Did you answer **every** bullet, and is the message clear? |
| **Grammatica** | 0–2 | Verb in the right place, correct simple sentences |
| **Spelling** | 0–2 | Words, names, dates spelled correctly |
| **Woordgebruik** (vocabulary) | 0–2 | Right words for the situation |
| **Samenhang** (coherence) | 0–1 | Linking words: *en, maar, want, omdat* |

Forms are judged only on **adequaatheid, spelling, woordgebruik**.

> **The one rule that fails people:** if **adequaatheid = 0**, the whole task = 0. Miss a required bullet, or write something the reader can't understand, and beautiful grammar won't save you. **Answer every bullet, in full sentences.**

## The winning strategy (do this every time)

1. **Read the bullets first.** Each task lists 2–4 things you must include (`•` points). Those bullets **are** the scoring rubric. Treat them as a checklist.
2. **Write one full sentence per bullet.** Not keywords — whole sentences (`Schrijf in hele zinnen` appears on every task). 3–5 sentences is plenty at A2.
3. **Keep the frame they give you.** Every e-mail already has the greeting (`Beste …`) and closing (`Groetjes,` / `Met vriendelijke groet,`). Write between them; don't remove them.
4. **Choose the right register:**
   - **u / formal** for a boss, teacher, doctor, landlord, the municipality → close with **Met vriendelijke groet**.
   - **je / informal** for a colleague, fellow student, neighbour, friend → close with **Groetjes / Groeten**.
5. **Verb in second position** in a main clause: *Ik **kan** morgen niet komen.* (not *Morgen ik kan…*). After **omdat / dat** the verb goes to the end: *…, **omdat** ik ziek **ben**.*
6. **Check the small facts before you submit:** names, dates, street names, phone number, e-mail address. These are cheap spelling points.
7. **Simple and correct beats long and wrong.** Short clear sentences score higher than ambitious ones with broken word order.

## Building blocks (phrase bank)

**Openers (why you are writing)**

- Ik schrijf u/je omdat … — *I'm writing because …*
- Ik heb een vraag. / Ik heb een probleem.
- Helaas kan ik … niet … — *Unfortunately I can't …*

**Making a request**

- Kunt u / Kun je … ?  Mag ik … ?  Is dat goed?

**Reason & linking**

- want / omdat (because) · en (and) · maar (but) · daarom (that's why) · ook (also)

**Time & dates**

- morgen, overmorgen, volgende week, donderdag 30 juli, om 15.00 uur

**Apology**

- Het spijt me (heel erg). Sorry voor het ongemak.

**Closings**

- Formal: **Met vriendelijke groet,** / Vriendelijke groet,
- Informal: **Groetjes,** / Groeten,
- Alvast bedankt! — *Thanks in advance!*

**Form words**

- Voor- en achternaam · Voorletters · Adres · Postcode · Woonplaats · Telefoonnummer · Geboortedatum · Geslacht: man / vrouw · Datum

## Common mistakes to avoid

- **Missing a bullet.** The fastest way to score 0 on a task. Tick each `•` off.
- **Keywords instead of sentences.** Every task says `Schrijf in hele zinnen`.
- **Wrong word order.** *Morgen ik kan niet komen* ❌ → *Morgen kan ik niet komen* ✅.
- **Deleting or ignoring the greeting/closing** the exam gives you.
- **Mixing u and je** in the same e-mail.
- **Sloppy data** in forms — a misspelled street name or wrong date loses easy points.
- **Writing too much.** More text = more mistakes. 3–5 clean sentences is the sweet spot.
- **Leaving a field blank.** Always write something plausible; empty = no points.

## Practice plan

1. Do all three practice booklets above **like the real exam** — timed, ~35 minutes, no dictionary.
2. Compare each answer to the samples here: did you hit **every bullet** in **full sentences**?
3. Reuse one invented identity so forms become automatic.
4. Drill the six e-mail openers/closings until you can write them without thinking.
5. Repeat the [Writing AI trainer](/#/writing) for instant feedback.
