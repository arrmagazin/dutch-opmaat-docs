**toen vs dan** (both "then"): use **toen** only for **past** events, **dan** for present/future and for sequencing.

- *Toen was ik nog jong.* — past: "back then I was still young."
- ❌ *Toen ga ik naar huis* (present) → ✅ *Dan ga ik naar huis* — *toen* is past-only; use *dan* for present/future.

**toen** marks a single event in the past — not a habit, not present/future (contrast **als** for habitual past / future condition, and **wanneer** for the question word).

- *Toen ik klein was, woonde ik in Den Haag.* — When I was little, I lived in The Hague.
