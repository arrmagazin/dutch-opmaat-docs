**daarom** gives a **motive** you act on; **daardoor** names an automatic cause-and-effect instead.

Watch the inversion: *Daarom **ging hij**…*, not ~~*Daarom hij ging*~~.

- *Hij was moe; **daarom** ging hij slapen.* — He was tired; that's why he went to sleep.

**daarom** and **daarna** belong to the same family as the er/daar-words — *daar* + preposition, frozen into a fixed adverb.
