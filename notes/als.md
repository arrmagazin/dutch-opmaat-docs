Use **als** (not *dan*!) for equality. *dan* is only for **in**equality — see [Comparatives](/#/grammar?doc=4-bijworden/36-comparatives.md).

- *Hij werkt **als** docent.* — He works as a teacher.

**Register note:** *groter als* is common in casual speech but considered non-standard — write and say **dan** in exams and formal Dutch.

**als** also covers a habitual past ("whenever") or a future condition ("if/when").

- *Als ik klein was, gingen we elke zomer kamperen.* — Whenever I was little, we went camping every summer.
- *Als ik tijd heb, kom ik.* — If I have time, I'll come.

Don't confuse **als** (if/when) with **of** (whether): "I don't know **if** he's coming" is *Ik weet niet **of** hij komt*, never *als*.
