**opdat** is a formal, written variant of **zodat** ("so that"); in everyday speech use **zodat**.

**om … te** vs **zodat** (both purpose): use **om … te** + infinitive when the subject stays the same; use **zodat** + full clause when the goal affects someone else.

**zo … dat** (result) is not **zodat** (purpose): *Het was **zo** koud **dat** ik bibberde* (result) vs *Spreek harder **zodat** ik je versta* (purpose).

- *Spreek harder **zodat** ik je **versta**.* — Speak louder so that I understand you.
