**waar** ("where") fuses with a preposition into an er-word (a **waar**-word) when asking about a thing: **met → mee**, **tot → toe** (*waarmee*, *waartoe*). The split form (*Waar wacht je **op**?*) is more natural in speech than the joined *Waarop wacht je?* To ask about an er-word, swap **er** for **waar**: *Waaraan denk je?*

- *Waar woon je?* — Where do you live?
