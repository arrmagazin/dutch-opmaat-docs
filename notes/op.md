**op** marks a flat, open surface — a table, the street, a bicycle; contrasted with **in** for a bounded space with walls. Learn the fixed *op*-cases: **op school, op het werk, op vakantie, op de fiets**, and islands (**op Texel, op Curaçao**).

- *De kop staat **op** tafel.* — The cup is on the table.

**op** also marks a specific day or date in time expressions — contrasted with **in** for months, years, and seasons.

- *Op **maandag** werk ik thuis.* — On Monday I work from home.
