**wanneer** asks "when?" — in a direct or an indirect question. It contrasts with **toen** (a single past event) and **als** (habitual past / future condition).

- *Wanneer kom je?* — When are you coming?
- *Ik weet niet wanneer hij komt.* — I don't know when he's coming.

**wanneer** also pairs with a preposition to bound a time span: **sinds wanneer** ("since when") and **tot wanneer** ("until when").

- *Sinds **wanneer** woon je hier?* — Since when do you live here?
