The same split hits **of**: *of* = "or" (coordinator) vs *of* = "whether" (subordinator).

- *Wil je koffie **of** thee?* — coordinator, "or".
- *Ik weet niet **of** hij **komt**.* — subordinator, "whether".
- ❌ *Wil je thee of koffie?* vs *Ik weet niet of hij komt* mixed up → *of* = "or" (coordinator) vs "whether" (subordinator).

Don't confuse **of** (whether) with **als** (if/when): "I don't know **if** he's coming" is *Ik weet niet **of** hij komt*, never *als*.
