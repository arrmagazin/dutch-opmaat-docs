**omdat** ties the cause in tightly to the clause it introduces; **want** merely adds a justification to an already finished thought.

- *Ik blijf thuis **omdat** ik ziek **ben**.* — I'm staying home because I'm sick.
- ❌ *…omdat ik **ben** moe* → ✅ *…omdat ik moe **ben*** — *omdat* sends the verb to the end.
