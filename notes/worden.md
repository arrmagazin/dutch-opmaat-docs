**worden** marks the passive of an ongoing action or process — the focus is on the action itself, not its result. In the past, **werd** signals the process was ongoing: *Het huis **werd** gebouwd* talks about the building process (e.g. in 1920), not the current state.

- *De brief **wordt** geschreven.* — The letter is being written.

Dutch has **no future with worden** — "I will go" is *Ik **zal** gaan* (with **zullen**), never ~~Ik word gaan~~, a classic German-interference trap.
