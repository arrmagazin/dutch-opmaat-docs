**niet** negates a definite noun, verb, adjective, adverb, or the whole sentence — contrasted with **geen** for an indefinite noun.

**Rule of thumb**: *de/het*-objects, pronouns, and time/place words go before **niet**; participles, infinitives, and prefixes go after it.

- *Ik zie de auto **niet**.* — I don't see the car.
