**over** marks a future time offset — "in X from now"; contrasted with **geleden** ("ago", a past offset that follows the time phrase) and **binnen** (any time within a period).

- *Ik bel je **over** vijf minuten.* — I'll call you in five minutes.

**over** — about [reference], introducing a topic.

- *Een boek **over** geschiedenis.* — A book about history.
