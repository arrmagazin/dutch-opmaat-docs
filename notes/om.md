**vragen om** = to request, to ask *for* something.

- *Ze vraagt **om** hulp.* — She asks for help.

**om** — at (clock time).

- *De film begint **om** acht uur.* — The movie starts at eight o'clock.

**om** also means "around" (spatially), a synonym of **rond** — distinct from its "at [clock time]" sense.

- *We zaten **rond** het vuur.* — We sat around the fire.
