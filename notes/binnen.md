**binnen** means "within" — any time up to a deadline, not necessarily exactly then; contrasted with **over** (a future offset) and **geleden** (a past offset).

- *Ik kom **binnen** een uur.* — I'll be there within an hour.

**binnen** also means "inside" (spatially) — contrasted with **buiten** for outside.

- *De kinderen spelen **buiten**.* — The kids play outside (as opposed to **binnen**, inside).
