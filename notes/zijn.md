**zijn** marks the passive of a completed result — the action is already finished and the state persists. In the passive perfect, **is** signals the finished state: *Het huis **is** gebouwd* — the current, finished state (it stands built today).

- *De brief **is** geschreven.* — The letter has been written.

**zijn** is also reserved for motion / change-of-state verbs (plus *zijn*, *blijven*, *worden*) as the perfect auxiliary; most other verbs take **hebben**.

- *Hij **is** gegaan.* — He has gone.
