**in** marks a bounded space with walls — a box, a country, a room; contrasted with **op** for a flat surface.

- *De boeken zitten **in** de tas.* — The books are in the bag.

**in** also marks months, years, and seasons in time expressions — contrasted with **op** for a specific day or date.

- *In **januari** sneeuwt het.* — It snows in January.
